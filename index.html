Science in VR

Interactive models of how the Earth system works, built to explore in a Meta Quest headset. They also run in an ordinary web browser.

Sunlight in, infrared out. Change the Sun, the albedo and the greenhouse effect and watch the planet warm or cool, with a step-by-step explainer.
